# Rush Ahead: Field Guide

Everything you can meet on the river, what it does and how to get past it. The same pages are in the game
(Field guide on the main menu, the pause menu and "What hit me?" on the results), and a card pops up the first
time you meet each one. Every round deals a fresh layout from the course's list, so you never quite know
what's round the next bend.

## On the river

### The edge

![The edge](guide/Edge.jpg)

**Go round.** The river runs along the top of a ridge with no banks to stop you. Drift past the stone lip and over you go.

*How to beat it:* Keep off the lip, especially on bends: the faster you go, the wider you swing.

*Where:* every course.

### Currents

![Currents](guide/Current.jpg)

**Use it.** Streaks of fast water. Arrows show which way they run: some speed you on, some push you sideways.

*How to beat it:* Ride the ones going your way; cut across the others at an angle.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Whirlpool

![Whirlpool](guide/Whirlpool.jpg)

**Go round.** A funnel of spinning water that drags rafts round and in towards its middle.

*How to beat it:* Skirt the rim on the side turning your way, or blow hard straight out of it.

*Where:* Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Boost lane

![Boost lane](guide/BoostLane.jpg)

**Use it.** Glowing chevrons on the water. Ride over them for a big surge of speed.

*How to beat it:* Line up early. They never send you faster than the next bend allows.

*Where:* every course.

### Rocks

![Rocks](guide/Rock.jpg)

**Go round.** Boulders standing in the river. Solid: a raft that hits one stops dead and wobbles.

*How to beat it:* Steer round; bounce off the foam ring, not the stone.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch.

### Barrels

![Barrels](guide/Barrel.jpg)

**Blow it away.** Loose barrels bobbing in the current. They bump you about.

*How to beat it:* Blow them out of the way with your air.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run.

### Drifting logs

![Drifting logs](guide/DriftingLog.jpg)

**Blow it away.** Loose logs drifting with the water, turning as they go.

*How to beat it:* Blow them aside, or slip past their ends.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Dynamite Gulch.

## Obstacles

### Paddle wheel

![Paddle wheel](guide/Wheel.jpg)

**Time it.** A big wooden wheel on a post, its paddles sweeping round across the river.

*How to beat it:* Pass between paddles, on the side sweeping the way you're going.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Thunder Rapids, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Crocodile

![Crocodile](guide/Crocodile.jpg)

**Dodge.** Lurks by the bank, then lunges across the river with its jaws open. A bite shoves and heels the raft.

*How to beat it:* Watch it coil back, then go behind it or wait for it to swim home. Bracing softens a bite.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Swinging log

![Swinging log](guide/SwingingLog.jpg)

**Brace.** A log hanging from a rope bridge, swinging across the river. Low enough to hit the crew at the bottom of its swing.

*How to beat it:* Brace together as it comes over: a braced crew ducks under it.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Geyser

![Geyser](guide/Geyser.jpg)

**Brace.** A vent in the riverbed. Bubbles rise, then it blasts a column of water that throws a raft up and outward.

*How to beat it:* Avoid the bubbling ring, or brace if you're on it when it goes.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Rockslide

![Rockslide](guide/Rockslide.jpg)

**Time it.** A crag on the bank with boulders balanced on top. One teeters, rolls down the scree chute and across the river.

*How to beat it:* Watch the top of the crag: when a boulder rocks, hang back or race past. Bracing helps a little.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Hippo

![Hippo](guide/Hippo.jpg)

**Brace.** Bubbles and two little ears, then a hippo heaves up under the water. Anything on top goes flying.

*How to beat it:* Steer round the bubbles; brace if you can't.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Thunder Rapids, Devil's Staircase.

### Sluice gate

![Sluice gate](guide/Sluice.jpg)

**Time it.** A timber wall across the river with one opening that slides from side to side.

*How to beat it:* Aim for where the gap is going, not where it is.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Sweeper

![Sweeper](guide/Sweeper.jpg)

**Time it.** A long log on a post in the river, spinning flat across the water.

*How to beat it:* Go through behind the sweep, or round the short side.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Water cannon

![Water cannon](guide/Cannon.jpg)

**Go round.** A cannon on the bank sweeps a jet of water across the river, shoving rafts towards the far edge.

*How to beat it:* Its aim line shows on the water before it fires. Pass behind the jet, or blow back against it.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Rapids

![Rapids](guide/Rapids.jpg)

**Ride it.** White water: boiling foam and standing waves that kick the raft about.

*How to beat it:* Go straight through and brace in the biggest waves.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Weir

![Weir](guide/Weir.jpg)

**Brace.** A step down in the river. You speed up over the lip and land hard below.

*How to beat it:* Square up and brace for the landing.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Bumper buoys

![Bumper buoys](guide/Bumper.jpg)

**Go round.** Springy buoys that bounce a raft away hard, sometimes towards the edge.

*How to beat it:* Give them room, and never bounce off one towards the lip.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Ferry

![Ferry](guide/Ferry.jpg)

**Time it.** A heavy cable ferry crossing back and forth. Solid and slow.

*How to beat it:* Pass behind it, never across its bow.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whitewater Run.

### Bear

![Bear](guide/Bear.jpg)

**Go round.** A bear on the bank swipes at rafts passing close, flinging them across the river.

*How to beat it:* Keep to the far side when you pass it.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Falling tree

![Falling tree](guide/FallingTree.jpg)

**Time it.** A dead tree on the bank creaks, shakes and crashes across the river once, as the first rafts come. Then it lies there with a gap past its crown.

*How to beat it:* If it's shaking, wait or race under it braced; once it's down, aim for the gap at its top.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Snapping turtle

![Snapping turtle](guide/Turtle.jpg)

**Go round.** A huge turtle paddling slowly across. Solid, and it snaps at rafts that come close, setting them spinning.

*How to beat it:* Pass well behind it.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Beaver Bend, Whitewater Run, Devil's Staircase.

### Piranha shoal

![Piranha shoal](guide/PiranhaShoal.jpg)

**Go round.** A reddish churn full of fins drifting down the river. Inside it you're nibbled: slowed and wobbled.

*How to beat it:* Steer round it; it moves with the water.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whitewater Run, Devil's Staircase.

### Waterspout

![Waterspout](guide/Waterspout.jpg)

**Dodge.** A spinning column of spray under a dark cloud, wandering across the river. It drags rafts in and flings them out.

*How to beat it:* Keep away from its foot; brace if it catches you.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Electric eels

![Electric eels](guide/ElectricEels.jpg)

**Time it.** Eels in a strip across the river. The water crackles blue, then pulses: blowers cut out for a second.

*How to beat it:* Cross between pulses, or brace through it.

*Where:* Reed Marsh, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Thunder Rapids, Devil's Staircase.

### Mudslide

![Mudslide](guide/Mudslide.jpg)

**Go round.** Brown slurry pours off a slope on the bank, shoving rafts across and slowing them.

*How to beat it:* Hug the far side while it flows.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Ice floes

![Ice floes](guide/IceFloes.jpg)

**Go round.** Big flat slabs of ice riding the current in a zig-zag. Solid: they block and squeeze.

*How to beat it:* Watch the gaps open and slip through.

*Where:* Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Icefall Chute.

### Beaver dam

![Beaver dam](guide/BeaverDam.jpg)

**Time it.** A dam across the river with three gaps the beavers open and shut in turn.

*How to beat it:* Head for the gap that's opening next.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Devil's Staircase.

### Diving pelican

![Diving pelican](guide/Pelican.jpg)

**Brace.** A pelican circles high, its shadow marks the spot, then it plunges. A hit knocks the raft over.

*How to beat it:* Steer off the shadow, or brace.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Devil's Staircase.

### Net boat

![Net boat](guide/NetBoat.jpg)

**Go round.** A fishing boat crosses dragging a net. Snagged rafts are dragged along with it.

*How to beat it:* Pass behind the net, not between the boat and its float.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whitewater Run, Dynamite Gulch.

### Kraken

![Kraken](guide/Kraken.jpg)

**Dodge.** A line of bubbles marks where a giant tentacle will rise from the bank, then it slams down across the water.

*How to beat it:* Steer off the line of bubbles; brace if it's coming down on you.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Whirlpool Reach, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Tidal bore

![Tidal bore](guide/TidalBore.jpg)

**Brace.** A wall of white water rolls up the river towards you, right across it.

*How to beat it:* Meet it square and braced, and you'll ride over it.

*Where:* Whirlpool Reach, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Jump ramp

![Jump ramp](guide/JumpRamp.jpg)

**Use it.** A log ramp in the river. Hit it fast and the raft flies, sailing over what's below.

*How to beat it:* Line up, go flat out, and brace for the landing.

*Where:* Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Goose gang

![Goose gang](guide/GooseGang.jpg)

**Blow it away.** Angry geese paddle out from the bank and chase rafts, pecking: you slow down and wobble.

*How to beat it:* Blow them away with your air, or outrun them: they can't keep up with a fast raft.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Reed Marsh, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Devil's Staircase.

### Chained mines

![Chained mines](guide/ChainedMines.jpg)

**Dodge.** Old spiked mines bobbing on chains, a red light blinking on each. Touch one and it goes off.

*How to beat it:* Go straight down the middle between them (their chains keep them in place), and brace if you're about to touch one.

*Where:* Whirlpool Reach, Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Wrecking ball

![Wrecking ball](guide/WreckingBall.jpg)

**Time it.** A huge iron ball swinging down and up the river from a gantry.

*How to beat it:* Go under the gantry just after the ball swings away from you.

*Where:* Whirlpool Reach, Glacier Gate, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Storm cloud

![Storm cloud](guide/StormCloud.jpg)

**Dodge.** A black cloud drifting over the river. A glowing ring marks each lightning strike just before it hits; a hit knocks the raft and, unless you're braced, cuts out its blowers.

*How to beat it:* Steer out of the glowing rings, or brace.

*Where:* Reed Marsh, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Paddle steamer

![Paddle steamer](guide/PaddleSteamer.jpg)

**Go round.** A big paddle boat churning along one half of the river. Its paddles knock and its wake rocks rafts behind.

*How to beat it:* Pass on the other half, well clear of the paddles.

*Where:* Mirror Lake, Red Rock Run, Dynamite Gulch.

### Mill race

![Mill race](guide/MillRace.jpg)

**Blow it away.** A mill on the bank draws a strong side current towards its turning wheel.

*How to beat it:* Keep to the far side and blow against the pull.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Drawbridge

![Drawbridge](guide/Drawbridge.jpg)

**Time it.** Two bridge leaves lower from the banks to meet over the river, then lift. Bells and lights warn first.

*How to beat it:* Go through while it's up, or through the gap as it opens.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Whitewater Run, Glacier Gate, Thunder Rapids, Icefall Chute, Devil's Staircase.

### Cargo crane

![Cargo crane](guide/CargoCrane.jpg)

**Dodge.** A crane on the bank swings out over the river and drops crates. Their shadows show where.

*How to beat it:* Steer off the shadows.

*Where:* Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Log flume

![Log flume](guide/LogFlume.jpg)

**Dodge.** A sawmill sends logs down a wooden chute into the river, where they bob along for a moment before they sink.

*How to beat it:* Watch the chute and pass between the logs.

*Where:* Beaver Bend, Red Rock Run, Thunder Rapids, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.

### Calving glacier

![Calving glacier](guide/CalvingGlacier.jpg)

**Brace.** An ice cliff on the bank cracks and drops a block. The splash sends a wave across the river.

*How to beat it:* Brace when the cliff cracks, before the wave reaches you.

*Where:* Glacier Gate, Icefall Chute.

### Dynamite float

![Dynamite float](guide/DynamiteFloat.jpg)

**Go round.** A crate of dynamite drifting down with its fuse fizzing. When the fuse runs out, it blows.

*How to beat it:* Keep well away from it until it's gone off.

*Where:* Red Rock Run, Dynamite Gulch.

### Leaping frogs

![Leaping frogs](guide/LeapingFrogs.jpg)

**Time it.** Giant frogs hop from lily pad to lily pad across the river. Each landing splashes rafts aside.

*How to beat it:* Pass between hops: they crouch just before they leap.

*Where:* Otter Creek, Mirror Lake, Reed Marsh, Thunder Rapids, Devil's Staircase.

### Giant fan

![Giant fan](guide/GiantFan.jpg)

**Blow it away.** A huge fan on the bank blows gusts across the river. The windsock shows when.

*How to beat it:* Blow back into it, or brace and hold your line.

*Where:* Whitewater Run, Red Rock Run, Icefall Chute, Dynamite Gulch, Devil's Staircase.
