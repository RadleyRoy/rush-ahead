# Rush Ahead privacy policy

*Last updated: 30 October 2026*

Rush Ahead is a party game made by Radley. This policy explains what the game does with information about you.
The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases, and we (the makers) don't receive
or keep anything about you.

## What stays on your phone

The game saves these on your device so they're there next time you play:

- the name you give your animal, and the animal, fur and blower you choose
- your settings (controls, sound, vibration, display)
- your best times and stars on each course
- a random number that identifies your phone to the game (not your phone's real ID), so that if your connection
  drops during a game you get your seat back when you rejoin

Uninstalling the game removes all of it. Settings > Reset best times clears your times.

## Playing together on Wi-Fi

When you host or join a game on the same Wi-Fi (or a phone's hotspot), the game sends your animal's name and look,
your random phone number and your controls directly to the other players' phones in that game. The game announces
hosted games on your local network so nearby players can find them. None of this leaves your local network.

## Playing online

Online games (joined with a code) run through Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies: Unity
Authentication signs the game in anonymously (there's no account, and no name, email or phone number is asked
for), Unity Lobby holds the game's join code, and Unity Relay passes the game's traffic between players, encrypted.
Unity therefore receives an anonymous player ID, your IP address, and the game's traffic (your animal's name and
look, your controls and the rafts' positions) while you play. Unity's handling of this is covered by its privacy
policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

We don't receive or store any of it ourselves.

## What the game doesn't do

It doesn't show ads, sell anything, track you across apps, use analytics, or ask for your location, contacts,
photos, camera or microphone. The only Android permissions it uses are for the network (to play together) and
vibration.

## Children

Rush Ahead is made for teenagers and adults, and isn't directed at children under 13.
